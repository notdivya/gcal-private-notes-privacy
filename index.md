# Google Calendar Private Meeting Notes – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 21, 2026*

## Data Collection
Google Calendar Private Meeting Notes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All notes you create are stored exclusively in your browser's local Chrome storage and never leave your device.

## Data Storage
Notes are stored locally on your device only. No data is sent to any external server, third party, or the developer.

## Permissions
- **storage**: Used solely to save and retrieve your notes locally on your device.
- **calendar.google.com host access**: Used solely to display the note-taking interface on Google Calendar pages. No calendar data is read or transmitted.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
